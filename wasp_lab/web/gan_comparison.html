Content Authenticity GAN: 4-Architecture Comparison Oxford-IIIT Pet · 128×128 · Tesla T4 · DiffAugment

WASP guarantees the privacy of a request (who asked for what), but nothing in its pipeline verifies the authenticity of content sitting in a router's cache. To demonstrate that gap, 4 GAN architectures were trained to synthesize fake cat/dog images — candidates for the fake content that gets deliberately injected into the mesh topology's caches as a simulated Content Poisoning Attack. Trained on ~7,349 real images (Oxford-IIIT Pet dataset, both splits combined), 100 epochs each, with DiffAugment regularization, cuDNN benchmark mode, and mixed-precision (AMP) training.

External Reference — DCGAN vs. WGAN-GP on CIFAR-10 Cited context, not our data

For independent context (not trained on our data): MannShah0312/WGAN-Wasserstein_Generative_Adversarial_Network compares the same DCGAN vs. WGAN-GP relationship on the standard CIFAR-10 benchmark. Our own result — WGAN-GP (256.68) and DCGAN (256.08) landing nearly tied — sits close to, but does not strongly confirm, WGAN-GP's usual stability advantage; the small gap here likely reflects how heavily both architectures benefited equally from DiffAugment and the fine-tuning regime, narrowing the difference the loss function alone would otherwise produce.
